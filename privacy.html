Undertone

Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

System audio

With your macOS permission, Undertone processes the output of apps you adjust so it can change their volume or mute them. Audio stays on your Mac and is used in real time. Undertone doesn’t keep audio recordings or send audio anywhere.

Preferences and data collection

Undertone saves per-app volume and mute preferences locally on your Mac. It doesn’t use advertising, analytics, or tracking, and the app doesn’t collect personal data. You can clear saved mixer settings with Reset All and remove audio access in System Settings.

Purchases

Apple handles payments and purchase restoration through the App Store. Undertone uses StoreKit to check whether the one-time unlock is available to your Apple Account. Apple handles payment information under its privacy policy.

Support

Messages you choose to submit to the public GitHub issue tracker are handled by GitHub and used to respond to your request. You can manage or remove the content you post there through GitHub where available. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

For questions about privacy or how to delete local preferences, use the Undertone support tracker.
