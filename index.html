Set up your mixer

Play audio in the app you want to adjust. In Undertone, drag that app’s slider or click its speaker button. The mixer follows the default output selected in macOS Sound settings.

Allow system audio

If macOS asks for system audio access, choose Allow. To enable it later, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording and add or enable Undertone under System Audio Recording Only. Return to Undertone and select Retry.

Restore your unlock

Open Unlock Undertone and select Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you used to make the purchase.

Report an issue

Share your macOS version, Undertone version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. The support issue tracker is public.
